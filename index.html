    Legal & Privacy

    This is not a real university

    Sticker Shock University is a made-up name for a free, personal planning tool. It is not a school, is not accredited, does not grant degrees and does not accept applications or payments. The crest, motto and mascot are original artwork created for this site.

    No affiliation or endorsement

    College names appear only to identify the schools being compared. This site is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any college, university, government agency or company it mentions. Chipotle and iPhone are trademarks of their owners and appear only as price comparisons.

    Estimates, not advice

    Every number here is an estimate for planning and conversation only. It is not financial, legal, tax or admissions advice. Prices change every year, and what a family actually pays depends on financial aid, scholarships, housing choices and other factors this tool does not know. Loan payments are simplified examples, not loan offers. Always confirm costs with each school's financial aid office and its official Net Price Calculator before making decisions. This site is provided as is, with no guarantee that any figure is accurate, complete or current, and its creator is not responsible for decisions made using it.

    Where the numbers come from

    The six starting schools use each school's own published 2026–27 cost of attendance. Schools added from the search use the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard data, a public-domain government source. Both are adjusted by an assumed 3% yearly increase.

    Privacy

    This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no ads and no tracking or analytics. It does not collect or send any personal information. Schools and numbers you enter are saved only in your own browser on your own device, and you can erase them any time with "Reset to the original six schools" or by clearing your browser's site data. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives the basic connection details any website visit sends, such as your IP address. The website host may keep standard server logs.
